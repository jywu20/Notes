Some notes in this folder use non-standard terminology,
like using "lexicalization" to denote lexical specification of *morphosyntactic* information.
Some terms are not inaccurate but may be misleading:
in many (most?) notes core/peripheral arguments mean arguments that are structurally close or far from the main verb, respectively,
while in typological literature the distinction is much more metaphorical and often means nothing more than whether a grammatical function frequently appears or not
(or is based on the nucleus/satellite theory of clause structure in some functional theories of syntax).
